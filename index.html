The Gardener

Every garden grows differently. So did I.

I like my garden with structure — thoughtfully planted, intentionally arranged, and always with a reason behind it.

That is how I approach my work: I like bringing different pieces together — connecting business, brand, consumers and execution to make an idea work in the real world.

But a good garden should never be too perfect.

I leave room for wildflowers — unexpected ideas, creative sparks and things worth exploring simply because they make me curious. And when the weather changes, I adapt, learn and keep growing.

Perhaps that is the kind of marketer I am: strategic enough to know where I'm going, curious enough to let something unexpected grow along the way.
